# Zeus and Athena House of Cosmetics Corp. — Design Reference

## Reference Video Design (Exact Match)
The reference video shows a modern, minimalist skincare e-commerce site with:
- Soft sage green background (#EDF1E8)
- Deep forest green (#2D3A30) for footer
- Clean beauty aesthetic with nature-inspired imagery
- Large typography with Cinzel serif for headings, DM Sans for body
- Asymmetric grid layouts with editorial magazine feel
- Scroll-triggered fade/slide animations
- Product carousels with shelf/niche card effect
- FAQ accordion with + icons and thin dividers
- Dark footer with massive outlined brand text
- "SKINCARE" as a watermark/category descriptor, NOT as the main brand mark

## Design Direction: Faithful Video Replication

### Core Principles
1. **Breathing Room** — Generous whitespace, editorial pacing
2. **Editorial Asymmetry** — Magazine-like grid, offset compositions, varied card sizes
3. **Typography as Voice** — Cinzel serif display for headings, DM Sans for body
4. **Unified Brand Identity** — "Zeus & Athena" is the brand mark; "SKINCARE" is only a watermark descriptor

### Color Philosophy
- **Background**: #EDF1E8 (soft sage green) — the calm, organic canvas
- **Text/Primary**: #1A1A1A (near-black) — authoritative, clean
- **Footer**: #2D3A30 (deep forest green) — grounded, luxurious
- **Accents**: Gold caps, botanical imagery — warmth and luxury

### Layout Paradigm
- Hero: Light bg with massive outlined watermark, vertical image center, floating card, Show Now button, rating
- Features: Two-column (text left, image right), numbered list, floating award badge, SINCE 2001 + LEARN MORE
- Products: Shelf/niche card effect with inset shadow, pill tab filters
- Categories: Pill tabs (NEW ARRIVAL/CLEANING/ACNE FIGHTER/ANTI-AGING), flat background grid
- Testimonial: Full-width image with woman and leaf, white centered overlay box
- FAQ: Left product jar image + 24/7 help badge, right accordion with + icons
- Footer: Dark green, newsletter CTA, social links, legal links, massive outlined brand text

### Typography System
- **Display Headings**: Cinzel (serif, uppercase, letter-spaced) — brand identity
- **Body & UI**: DM Sans (sans-serif) — clean, modern
- **Watermark**: Cinzel Extra Bold, massive scale, transparent with stroke

### Brand Identity
- **Navbar**: "Zeus & Athena" text logo (uppercase, letter-spaced)
- **Footer**: "Join The Zeus & Athena Community Now" + massive outlined "Zeus & Athena" text
- **Watermark**: "Zeus & Athena" as the brand watermark on hero
- **SKINCARE**: Only appears as a category descriptor or watermark, never as the main brand mark

### Brand Voice
- Headlines: Confident, poetic simplicity
- Body copy: Warm, educational, ingredient-transparent
- CTAs: Action-oriented but refined

### Style Decisions
- Framer Motion for scroll-triggered animations
- Sticky navigation with dark background
- Mobile-first responsive: sm(640), md(768), lg(1024), xl(1280)
- Separate TSX files for each section component
- No generic filler copy — all text branded and specific
- Product cards use shelf/niche inset shadow effect
- Pill-shaped category tabs with active state
- FAQ uses + icon accordion with thin dividers
- Footer has massive outlined brand text at bottom
